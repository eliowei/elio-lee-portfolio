## Editorial standards

The technical notes start from real problems and follow the order "reproduce → find the cause → fix and verify → prevent":

- **Experience vs general explanation**: personal implementation experience states its context clearly; general technical explanations are checked against official documentation and specifications where possible.
- **Verifiable code**: examples are kept runnable where possible, with their assumptions and version differences explained.
- **No confidential information**: work-related content only describes approaches and ideas, never company code, data or internal information.
- **Dated articles**: every article shows its publication date, so you can judge whether it may be out of date.

## Reporting a mistake

If you find something wrong, outdated or unclear in an article, please write to me through the [Contact me](/en/contact) page, choose the "Content correction" topic, and include:

1. The article's URL
2. The passage in question
3. What you believe is correct, or a source I can refer to

I will reply within 24–48 hours.

## How corrections are made

- Once a mistake is confirmed, I correct the article directly.
- For significant corrections that change a conclusion, the article explains what was changed.
- The English and Japanese translations are updated at the same time.

Thank you for helping make these notes more accurate.
