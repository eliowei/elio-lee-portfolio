This is the personal portfolio and technical notes site of Elio Lee (李柏偉). This policy explains what data the site comes into contact with, what it is used for, and your rights.

## What this site does not do

- It doesn't use analytics tools such as Google Analytics.
- It doesn't set tracking cookies or store your personal data in your browser.
- It doesn't show ads, and never sells or shares any visitor data.

## The contact form

When you send a message through the [Contact me](/en/contact) page, the form collects:

- Your name
- Your email address
- The topic
- Your message

This data is sent to my inbox through [EmailJS](https://www.emailjs.com/). It is only used to reply to your message, is never used for marketing, and is never given to third parties. The email stays in my inbox until you ask me to delete it, or until I no longer need it.

## Third-party services

The site relies on the following services, which may process your browsing information (such as your IP address and browser type) under their own policies:

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Vercel | Hosts the site. Keeps access logs for operations and security. |
| Google Fonts | Loads the site's fonts and icons. Your browser requests the font files from Google's servers. |
| EmailJS | Sends the contents of the contact form. |

The site also links to external sites such as GitHub and project demos. Once you leave this site, that site's privacy policy applies.

## Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete the data you provided through the contact form. Please write to me through the [Contact me](/en/contact) page and say what you need.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.
